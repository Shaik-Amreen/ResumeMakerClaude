================================================================================
HYGIENE — NO INVENTED FACTS
================================================================================
- No invented facts, metrics, scope, employers, or URLs. If no number exists in truth sources,
  leave it out — never fabricate one.
- Amazon details = LinkedIn-public only (Lambda/DynamoDB/SQS, CDK/CI/CD/CloudWatch, AI assistant skill).
- Never invent or change live project URLs.
- NEVER use em-dashes or en-dashes (Unicode or LaTeX `--` / `---`). They render as long dashes in the PDF.
- Use a plain ASCII hyphen with spaces on BOTH sides: " - " (e.g. 05/2026 - 08/2026, NativeNest - Cross-Platform, Developer - 2024).
- Never write "–2024" or "01/2025 –01/2027" (missing space after the dash).
- Compound words keep a single tight hyphen with no spaces (Cross-Platform, blue-green).
- Never Unicode em/en dashes or smart quotes in prose.
